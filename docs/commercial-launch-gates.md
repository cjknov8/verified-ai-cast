# Commercial Launch Gates

The service must remain in demonstration mode until all required gates report
ready at `/api/release-readiness`.

## Technical

- Production database and migrations applied
- Supabase Auth with server-side role enforcement
- Private evidence storage with signed URLs
- Certificate signing key isolated from the web deployment
- Error, availability, audit, and security monitoring
- Backup, restore, key rotation, and incident response drills
- Rate limits, abuse controls, and webhook replay protection

## Trust Operations

- Seller KYC/KYB and buyer verification vendor selected
- Authority-review checklist and reviewer training
- Dual control for high-value issuance and revocation
- Fraud, impersonation, mismatch, takedown, and appeal procedures
- Published response targets and emergency suspension process
- Named trust-and-safety and security owners

## Payments

- Merchant and payout accounts approved
- Escrow or conditional-release legal model confirmed
- Refund, chargeback, tax, sanctions, and payout-hold procedures
- Idempotent webhook processing and ledger reconciliation
- Buyer-remedy wording approved before offering an authenticity guarantee

## Legal and Privacy

- Operating legal entity and launch jurisdictions selected
- Counsel-approved terms, privacy, certificate policy, relying-party policy,
  content policy, refund policy, dispute policy, and seller/buyer agreements
- Data map, retention schedule, subprocessors, cross-border transfer mechanism,
  and sensitive-data assessment completed
- AI disclosure, publicity/personality, trademark, copyright, consumer, and
  electronic-signature requirements approved per jurisdiction

## Commercial Pilot

Launch first as a controlled pilot:

1. One jurisdiction
2. One asset category
3. Verified sellers by invitation
4. Manual authority review
5. Transaction and certificate value limits
6. No broad authenticity guarantee until claims data and insurance exist
7. Ten to twenty monitored releases before public self-service
