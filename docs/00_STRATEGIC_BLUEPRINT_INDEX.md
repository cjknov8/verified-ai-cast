## Strategic Direction

The current product is Phase 1 of a larger company:

1. **Verified AI Cast**: approval and certification for performers.
2. **Verified Productions**: verified projects, studios, and production entities.
3. **Verified Brand Placements**: approved use of brands, products, and trade dress.
4. **Verified Rights Registry**: a cross-category registry of authorized digital usage.
5. **AI Entertainment Trust Infrastructure**: an industry verification and interoperability layer.

The company does not need to control or replace generation tools. Its durable role is to answer:

> Who authorized this specific use, under what authority and terms, for which content and distribution locations, and is that authorization still valid?

## Board-Level Decisions

The company should make five decisions early:

1. **Stay narrow before expanding.** Win performer approvals and public certificates before adding broad IP categories.
2. **Separate identity, authority, approval, and content integrity.** Each proves a different fact and must never be collapsed into one badge.
3. **Be a registry, not a marketplace.** Transactions may be supported, but public legitimacy and durable records are the primary product.
4. **Adopt institutional governance before claiming authority.** Transparent rules, appeals, audits, key management, and revocation are prerequisites.
5. **Use Verified AI Cast as a product name, not necessarily the permanent company name.** The long-term parent brand must work for performers, brands, characters, game IP, and enterprise infrastructure.

## Non-Negotiable Product Principles

- No raw likeness, voice model, embedding, or reusable identity asset marketplace by default.
- No certificate without verified authority and a recorded approval act.
- No active certificate without a defined subject, content version, scope, term, and status.
- Revoked and expired records remain publicly inspectable.
- A certificate is evidence of platform-recorded authorization, not a universal legal opinion.
- Content provenance supports authorization evidence but does not replace it.
- Public language must distinguish `verified identity`, `verified authority`, `approved use`, and `content match`.

## Success Definition

The company succeeds when an advertiser, platform, studio, agency, journalist, or viewer checks the registry before relying on a digital-rights claim, and when rights holders expect legitimate projects to appear there.
