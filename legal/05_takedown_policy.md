# Takedown Policy

> Product policy draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice.

## Scope

This process covers certificate misuse, publication outside approved URLs, revoked or expired approval claims, impersonation, unsafe content, and rights-holder complaints.

## Intake

Collect:

- reporter identity and contact
- affected actor or agency
- certificate ID, if present
- published URLs and screenshots
- media copy or hash
- reason for complaint
- urgency and safety risk

## Triage

| Priority | Example | Initial action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | fraud, intimate imagery, minors, immediate safety risk | suspend certificate display, preserve evidence, escalate immediately |
| High | certificate reused on unapproved URL, revoked approval still displayed | show mismatch or revoked state, notify creator, request removal |
| Standard | scope dispute, metadata error, ordinary complaint | investigate and respond through case record |

## Process

1. Open a case and preserve evidence.
2. Confirm certificate state and approved URL allowlist.
3. Notify the creator unless urgent safety handling requires immediate restriction.
4. Record platform actions in the audit log.
5. Revoke or suspend certification when justified.
6. Request removal from the creator, host, platform, or CDN as appropriate.
7. Notify the actor or agency of resolution.

## Records

Keep complaint, evidence, communications, decisions, timestamps, and certificate history according to a counsel-approved retention schedule.

## Appeals

Provide an appeal channel for factual error, authority disputes, and restored rights. Appeals do not automatically reactivate a certificate.
