# Regulatory Research Notes

> Product research draft for counsel review, last reviewed on 2026-06-01. This is not legal advice. Confirm current law, territorial scope, and effective dates with qualified counsel before launch.

## Product Relevance

Verified AI Cast should be designed as an approval, disclosure, and revocation system. It should not claim that a certificate alone resolves copyright, publicity, privacy, advertising, labor, or platform-policy obligations.

## South Korea

The Korean Unfair Competition Prevention and Trade Secret Protection Act includes a provision addressing unauthorized commercial use of identifiers such as another person's name, portrait, voice, or signature where the identifier is widely recognized domestically and has economic value.

Product implications:

- retain explicit actor or agency authority records
- bind approval to scope, term, territory, and URLs
- prohibit misleading commercial reuse
- preserve revocation and complaint history

Official source:

- [Korean National Law Information Center, Act text](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?ancYnChk=0&chrClsCd=010202&efYd=20251001&lsiSeq=277201&urlMode=lsInfoP)

## European Union

EU Regulation 2024/1689, Article 50 includes transparency obligations for certain AI-generated or manipulated content. It states that deployers of AI systems generating or manipulating image, audio, or video constituting a deep fake shall disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated. Article 113 states the Regulation generally applies from 2026-08-02, with listed exceptions.

Product implications:

- require disclosure placement during review
- retain disclosure requirements in talent policy
- separate official actor approval from statutory AI disclosure
- plan for machine-readable provenance support

Official source:

- [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1689)

## United States

The U.S. Copyright Office published its July 2024 report on digital replicas and recommended federal legislation. The report defines the policy problem around realistic digitally created or manipulated depictions of individuals and discusses licensing, liability, remedies, and interaction with state law.

The FTC has also addressed impersonation harms. Its Government and Business Impersonation Rule is in effect, and the FTC separately proposed expanding protection concerning impersonation of individuals. Counsel must confirm the status of any individual-impersonation rulemaking at launch.

Product implications:

- do not present this service as a substitute for state-law review
- prohibit deceptive endorsements and impersonation
- retain evidence for takedown and dispute handling
- review state publicity, digital replica, labor, and advertising rules by launch territory

Official sources:

- [U.S. Copyright Office AI initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/AI/)
- [U.S. Copyright Office Digital Replicas Report](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-1-Digital-Replicas-Report.pdf)
- [FTC Government and Business Impersonation Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/impersonation-government-businesses-rule)
- [FTC proposed protections concerning impersonation of individuals](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/02/ftc-proposes-new-protections-combat-ai-impersonation-individuals)

## Provenance Standards

C2PA Content Credentials provide a technical framework for cryptographically bound provenance assertions, manifests, signatures, validation, and credential revocation checks.

Product implications:

- evaluate C2PA as a complementary media-integrity layer
- keep Verified AI Cast certificates as rights-holder approval records
- avoid claiming that provenance metadata alone proves legal authorization

Official source:

- [C2PA Technical Specification](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html)
