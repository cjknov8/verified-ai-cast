# Content Policy

> Product policy draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice.

## Principle

Official certification is discretionary. A creator may submit an AI-generated result, but no result receives official status until it passes policy review and actor or agency approval.

## Always Reject

- Sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, or content involving minors
- Fraud, impersonation scams, phishing, or deceptive identity use
- Hate, harassment, threats, or incitement
- Illegal goods, criminal facilitation, or evasion of law enforcement
- Publication of private addresses, family details, or sensitive personal data without authorization
- Attempts to reuse a certificate for an unapproved result or URL

## Require Explicit Escalation

- Political, religious, medical, financial, gambling, or legal endorsements
- Voice cloning, singing, or unscripted dialogue
- Romance, kissing, family, or private relationship simulation
- Violence, substance abuse, criminality, or reputationally sensitive contexts
- Material age changes, body changes, or misleading claims of real-world conduct
- New territory, platform, brand category, derivative edit, or publishing URL

## Required Submission Materials

- Final media file
- Transcript and subtitles
- Prompt and tool log
- Intended use and channels
- Territory and release window
- Revenue model
- Requested actor-name use
- Proposed publishing URLs

## Enforcement

The platform may reject, request changes, suspend review, revoke a certificate, preserve evidence, restrict an account, and initiate the takedown process.
