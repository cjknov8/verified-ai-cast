# Agency Agreement Outline

> Product policy draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice and is not ready for signature.

## Purpose

Define agency reviewer authority and operating duties for official AI appearance approvals.

## Outline

1. **Representation authority**
   - Identify represented talent and the scope of agency authority.
   - Require updates when representation changes.

2. **Reviewer accounts**
   - Named reviewers, least-privilege access, MFA requirement, and removal of departed personnel.

3. **Policy administration**
   - Maintain actor restrictions, commercial guardrails, SLA targets, and disclosures.

4. **Decision authority**
   - Approve, approve with limits, reject, request changes, and revoke within delegated authority.
   - Escalate decisions outside delegation to the actor or platform operator.

5. **Audit trail**
   - Record reviewer identity, timestamp, reason, submission version, certificate ID, and approved URLs.

6. **Incident handling**
   - Report impersonation, certificate reuse, unsafe output, account compromise, and rights disputes.

7. **Financial administration**
   - Review fees, license payments, payout instructions, tax records, and disputed amounts.

8. **Termination**
   - Reviewer access removal, active certificate review, surviving records, and open takedowns.

## Counsel Questions

- What proof of agency authority is required?
- Which decisions require actor countersignature?
- How should authority changes affect existing certificates?
