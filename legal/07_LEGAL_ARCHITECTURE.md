# Integrated Legal Architecture

> Strategic drafting outline for qualified counsel. Not legal advice, not jurisdiction-complete, and not ready for signature.

## Legal Product Principle

The registry should preserve evidence of identity, authority, agreement, approval, scope, content, publication, status, and subsequent events. It should not represent that registry evidence eliminates conflicting rights, statutory exceptions, labor obligations, or judicial review.

### Platform-Level Agreements

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Acceptable Use and Content Policy
- Electronic Records and Signature Consent
- Verification and Relying-Party Policy

## Core Clauses Across Agreements

### Rights Ownership and Reservation

- No transfer of identity ownership by platform onboarding.
- No implied ownership determination by registry entry.
- Each party identifies the legal basis for rights it claims or grants.
- All rights not expressly granted remain reserved.

### Likeness and Voice

- Separate, explicit treatment of name, image, likeness, voice, motion, biometric inputs, and digital replicas.
- Specific intended uses, media, territory, term, and modification rights.
- No model training, reusable model creation, or asset extraction unless separately authorized.

### Usage Scope

- exact project and submission version
- permitted edits and derivatives
- channel and platform
- territory
- effective and expiration dates
- paid media and organic media
- actor-name or brand-name promotional use
- archival use
- disclosure and certificate placement

### Commercial Rights

- license fee
- review fee
- royalties or performance-based compensation
- platform fees
- reporting and audit
- taxes, withholding, refunds, and chargebacks
- treatment after revocation or expiration

### Revocation and Suspension

Distinguish:

- contractual termination
- certificate suspension
- certificate revocation
- license termination
- emergency platform restriction
- expiration

Define grounds, notice, cure, takedown timing, downstream obligations, financial consequences, and appeal.

### Death, Incapacity, and Estate Handling

- participant instructions and nominated contacts
- verification of executor, trustee, heir, successor, or licensee
- jurisdiction-specific postmortem rights
- active certificate treatment
- authority disputes
- memorial and archival use
- no automatic assumption that an agency remains authorized

### Evidence and Audit

- electronic records consent
- signer authentication
- timestamps
- content hashes and manifests
- policy and agreement versions
- reviewer identity and delegation
- retention period
- admissibility language where appropriate
- export and legal hold

## Actor Agreement Additions

The existing outline should add:

- informed digital-replica consent by category
- biometric and source-material handling
- model-training prohibition by default
- delegation matrix for agency decisions
- performer countersignature thresholds
- emergency suspension rights
- future incapacity and estate instructions
- union or collective bargaining precedence
- audit and usage reporting rights

## Agency Agreement Additions

- representation warranties by right and territory
- authority start and end dates
- duty to notify changes immediately
- conflict checks
- named reviewer and delegation controls
- no commingling of agency approval with performer consent where both are required
- transition duties after representation ends
- liability allocation for false authority claims

## Creator License Additions

- chain-of-title warranty for all submitted materials
- disclosure of generation tools and material transformations
- no reliance on certificate outside stated scope
- no content substitution at an approved URL
- reporting of mirrors, embeds, reposts, and platform IDs
- cooperation with fingerprinting and monitoring
- immediate cessation of active-approval claims after status change
- records and revenue reporting

## Content Policy Architecture

Use three levels:

- **Prohibited**: never certified.
- **Restricted**: requires enhanced rights-holder and platform review.
- **Rights-holder policy**: individually configurable within law and platform rules.

The policy should cover non-consensual intimate content, minors, fraud, deceptive endorsements, hate, harassment, political persuasion, medical and financial claims, gambling, violence, illegal goods, private data, biometric extraction, and certificate misuse.

## Takedown and Dispute Architecture

Create separate case types:

- certificate misuse
- unapproved content or URL
- authority dispute
- ownership dispute
- impersonation or fraud
- safety emergency
- privacy or data rights request
- legal demand
- correction request

The platform may suspend a certificate without deciding ultimate ownership when continued reliance would create material risk.
