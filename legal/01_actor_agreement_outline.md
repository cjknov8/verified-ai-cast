# Actor Agreement Outline

> Product policy draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice and is not ready for signature.

## Purpose

Define how an actor authorizes the platform and an agency to review, approve, reject, limit, and revoke official AI appearance certifications.

## Outline

1. **Parties and authority**
   - Actor identity, representative agency, platform entity, territory.
   - Confirmation that the signer has authority to grant the covered rights.

2. **No blanket asset sale**
   - The agreement does not transfer ownership of the actor's identity.
   - Face, voice, likeness, reference media, and model-related files are not downloadable marketplace assets unless separately agreed in writing.

3. **Approval scope**
   - Each approval applies only to a named project, reviewed result version, usage scope, validity window, and approved publishing URLs.
   - Actor-name use and official approval marks are conditional on an active certificate.

4. **Policy controls**
   - Restricted categories, geography, channels, brands, voice cloning, age simulation, and disclosure requirements.
   - Manual review requirement for public release.

5. **Review actions**
   - Approve, approve with limited scope, reject, request changes, revoke.
   - Required reasons and response time targets.

6. **Revocation**
   - Grounds, notice process, urgent suspension process, downstream removal obligations, and settlement treatment.

7. **Audit and records**
   - Consent to retain append-only decision records, certificate history, approved URLs, timestamps, and reviewer identity.

8. **Compensation**
   - Review fees, license fees, royalties, payout schedule, withholding, refunds, and disputed amounts.

9. **Data handling**
   - Private review media access, storage term, security controls, deletion procedures, and incident notification.

10. **Term and termination**
    - Effect on active certificates, survival of audit records, and outstanding payment obligations.

## Counsel Questions

- Which rights must be expressly licensed by jurisdiction?
- Can an agency revoke on behalf of an actor?
- What notice period is enforceable for active campaigns?
- What audit data must be retained after termination?
