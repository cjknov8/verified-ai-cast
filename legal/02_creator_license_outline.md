# Creator License Outline

> Product policy draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice and is not ready for signature.

## Purpose

Define a limited, conditional license for a creator to publish an approved AI appearance and display an official certificate.

## Outline

1. **Submission warranty**
   - Creator has rights to submit all media, scripts, prompts, references, music, and supporting files.
   - Creator discloses material AI generation and manipulation methods.

2. **Review package**
   - Final media version, transcript, prompt/tool log, publishing channels, revenue model, territory, release window, and requested actor-name usage.

3. **Conditional license**
   - License begins only after approval and certificate issuance.
   - License applies only to the reviewed version, approved scope, term, territory, channels, and approved URLs.

4. **Certificate display**
   - Creator may display the certificate link and mark only on approved URLs.
   - Reuse on a different URL, domain, edit, campaign, or derivative work requires review.

5. **Required disclosures**
   - AI-generated or manipulated appearance disclosure.
   - Certificate URL placement and any actor-specific disclosure rules.

6. **Prohibited conduct**
   - Misleading endorsement, sublicensing, certificate-link reuse, removal of disclosures, evasion of revocation, and publication of rejected versions.

7. **Revision and takedown**
   - Response deadlines for revision requests, urgent suspension, revocation, and removal.

8. **Fees and reporting**
   - License fee, review fee, royalties, revenue reporting, taxes, chargebacks, and audit rights.

9. **Remedies**
   - Certificate deactivation, takedown request, suspension, indemnity, dispute escalation, and preservation of evidence.

## Counsel Questions

- Define fair cure periods by channel and territory.
- Confirm platform liability limits and creator indemnity scope.
- Confirm treatment of embeds, mirrors, social reposts, and CDN URLs.
