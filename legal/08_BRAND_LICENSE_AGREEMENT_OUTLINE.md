# Brand License Agreement Outline

> Counsel-review outline only. Not legal advice and not ready for signature.

## Purpose

Define a narrow authorization for the depiction, modification, placement, and promotion of specified brand assets in a named production and approved distribution scope.

## Outline

1. **Parties and authority**
   - Brand owner, licensing entity, agency, production company, and platform.
   - Evidence that the signer controls the relevant marks, product assets, and approval rights.

2. **Brand assets**
   - Trademark, logo, product, packaging, trade dress, design, archival material, digital twin, or other asset.
   - Product model, season, colorway, and approved reference material.

3. **No general asset license**
   - No model training, extraction, resale, sublicensing, template creation, or reusable generation right unless expressly stated.

4. **Approved production**
   - Named project, submission version, scene, script, context, performers, characters, and co-brands.

5. **Modification controls**
   - Permitted transformations, product accuracy, prohibited alterations, logo treatment, synthetic features, claims, and visual quality.

6. **Commercial scope**
   - Channels, territory, term, paid media, product pages, social use, OOH, broadcast, streaming, and events.

7. **Exclusivity and conflicts**
   - Product category, competitor, performer, territory, and campaign conflicts.

8. **Approval process**
   - Submission package, reviewer authority, response target, revisions, final lock, and conditions.

9. **Certificate and registry**
   - Public or private record, certificate mark, approved URLs and platform IDs, status, expiration, revocation, and archival display.

10. **Disclosure**
    - AI or synthetic-content disclosure, paid placement disclosure, endorsement limitations, and jurisdiction-specific wording.

11. **Monitoring**
    - Publication verification, content fingerprinting, media monitoring, reporting, and audit rights.

12. **Revocation and takedown**
    - Brand-safety event, content substitution, scope breach, legal issue, performer dispute, urgent suspension, cure period, and removal.

13. **Fees**
    - License, review, monitoring, platform, production, royalty, and reporting terms.

14. **Quality and reputation**
    - Objective brand guidelines, material breach standards, and avoidance of unconstrained morality clauses where local law requires precision.

15. **IP ownership**
    - Brand ownership, production copyright, approved derivative rights, certificate data, and feedback.

16. **Warranties and indemnities**
    - Authority, non-infringement, legal compliance, product claims, and third-party materials.

17. **Confidentiality and launch controls**
    - embargo, private review, unreleased product information, leaks, and delayed public registry publication.

18. **Term and survival**
    - expiration, archival evidence, outstanding payments, audit, confidentiality, and dispute records.

## Required Certificate Fields

- brand legal entity
- authorized representative
- brand asset and product identifiers
- production and final submission version
- approved context
- approved locations
- territory and term
- disclosure requirements
- status
- related performer and production certificates
