## Relying-Party Policy

Anyone relying on a certificate must:

- check current status
- verify the applicable content and location
- read the scope and assurance level
- avoid interpreting the certificate as a general endorsement
- not rely after expiration, suspension, revocation, or supersession
- report suspected misuse

The platform should state the maximum staleness permitted for cached status.

## Privacy Policy

### Data Categories

- account and contact data
- identity verification data
- business and authority records
- performer and biometric-related source materials
- contracts and signatures
- project and media files
- certificate and public registry data
- monitoring and publication observations
- payment and tax data
- device, log, and security data
- support, dispute, and legal records

### Purposes

- identity and authority verification
- contract and approval workflow
- certificate issuance and validation
- fraud prevention and security
- payments and reporting
- monitoring and dispute handling
- legal compliance
- service improvement with strict limits on sensitive material

### Critical Commitments

- Do not train general-purpose generative models on private performer or brand materials without separate express agreement.
- Do not sell raw identity verification or biometric data.
- Separate public registry data from confidential evidence.
- Apply role-based access and short-lived media access.
- Publish retention periods by record type.
- Preserve only necessary audit evidence after account closure.

### Rights and International Issues

- access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and appeal as applicable
- biometric and sensitive-data consent where required
- processor and subprocessors
- cross-border transfer mechanism
- children and minors
- estate requests and deceased-person data distinctions
- automated decision-making disclosures if risk scoring has legal or significant effects

## Content Policy

The existing content policy should be expanded with:

- non-consensual digital replicas
- biometric extraction or model inversion
- deceptive commercial endorsement
- unlawful discrimination
- election and political persuasion rules
- unsafe impersonation and fraud
- unlicensed character, brand, or product claims
- attempts to create false registry evidence

## Takedown Policy

The existing takedown policy should add:

- standard notice fields by complaint type
- counter-notice or response process where appropriate
- authority and ownership dispute track
- emergency certificate suspension
- content preservation and legal hold
- repeat abuser policy
- notification to relying parties
- public status wording during investigation
- appeal and independent review for high-impact cases

## Retention Schedule Categories

- identity verification evidence
- authority evidence
- executed agreements
- rejected submissions
- approved content and manifests
- certificate and audit history
- payments and tax
- monitoring observations
- security logs
- disputes and legal holds

Counsel and security should define periods by jurisdiction and purpose; "keep everything forever" is not a valid registry policy.
